Type "find my AI business idea" or `/abig` and this skill walks you through five stages: a quick Ikigai interview, 2-3 idea cards to pick from, 10-20 live research searches for real competitors and prices, the Offer Triangle (what, who, price), and a self-contained HTML report with a 7-day action plan.

No business experience needed — it treats any past job, hobby, or side project as credibility for your offer, and refuses generic "AI consulting" answers.

Created by [Patrick Dang](https://www.youtube.com/c/PatrickDangOfficial/about) — all credit goes to him. Enable web search in Claude before running it, since the research stages depend on it.
